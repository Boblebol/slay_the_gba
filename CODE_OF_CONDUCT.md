# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
*Ce code s'applique dans toutes les langues.*

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Slay the GBA (issues, pull requests, discussions, any space linked to the project) to be a
harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

* Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a language that is not their own.
* Give and take feedback on the work, not on the person.
* Accept responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
* Focus on what is best for the project and its players.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
* Insults, trolling, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
* Public or private harassment.
* Publishing someone's private information (address, e-mail…) without their explicit permission.
* Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a friendly community.

## Enforcement

The maintainer ([@Boblebol](https://github.com/Boblebol)) is responsible for enforcing this code and may remove,
edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow it, and temporarily or
permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful.

Report unacceptable behaviour to [alexandre.enouf@gmail.com](mailto:alexandre.enouf@gmail.com). Every report is
reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Depending on the case, the answer goes from a private warning to a
permanent ban from the project's spaces.
