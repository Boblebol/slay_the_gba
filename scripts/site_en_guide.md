# How to play

Slay the GBA is a roguelike deckbuilder: you climb the Spire with a small deck of cards, fight your way up three
acts and grow stronger with every card, relic and potion you pick up. Every run is different, and a defeat sends you
back to the start. The game itself is in French; this guide uses the English names of Slay the Spire 2.

## A run, from start to finish

1. **Pick your cartridge.** Each of the five ROMs is one hero, with their own starting deck, starting relic and
   signature mechanic. On the title screen, choose *Nouvelle partie* (new run), then the hero's colour and, once
   unlocked, the Ascension level.
2. **Neow greets you.** Every run opens on Neow, who offers 2 blessings and 1 relic with a price: take one before
   the first map.
3. **Climb the act.** Each act is a branching map of 16 floors. You pick your path room by room, up to the boss at
   the top.
4. **Beat the boss.** After the act 1 and act 2 bosses you get a Rare relic, cards and gold, and heal between acts.
5. **Meet an Ancient.** At the start of acts 2 and 3 an Ancient offers you 3 relics: take one. Their gifts are
   among the most powerful items of a run.
6. **Win the run** by beating the act 3 boss. The credits roll, and the hero unlocks the next Ascension level.

## Rooms on the map

| Room | What happens |
|---|---|
| ⚔️ Monster | A normal fight. Rewards: gold, one card out of three (or skip), sometimes a potion. |
| 😈 Elite | A much harder fight. Rewards: a relic, more gold and better cards. |
| 🔥 Campfire | **Rest** to heal 30% of your max HP, or **Smith** to upgrade a card for good (with a preview). |
| 🎁 Treasure | A chest with gold and a relic. |
| 💰 Merchant | Buy cards, relics and potions, or pay to remove a card from your deck (the price goes up each time). |
| ❓ Event | A short story with choices: risks, trades, curses and rewards. |
| 👑 Boss | The guardian of the act. |

## Combat

- You get **3 energy** per turn and draw **5 cards**. Each card costs energy (the number in its corner).
- **Attacks** deal damage, **Skills** mostly give Block or utility, **Powers** stay in effect until the end of the
  fight.
- **Block** absorbs damage and is reset at the start of your turn.
- Above every enemy, its **intent** shows what it will do next turn: attack (with the damage), defend, buff or
  debuff. Plan around it.
- Key statuses: **Strength** adds damage to every attack, **Dexterity** adds Block, **Vulnerable** makes the target
  take 50% more damage, **Weak** makes it deal 25% less, **Poison** hurts at the start of its turn and goes down by 1.
- Cards you play go to the Discard Pile, which is shuffled back into the Draw Pile when it runs out. **Exhausted**
  cards are gone until the end of the fight.
- You carry up to **3 potions**. Drink one during your turn with the potion belt button.

## Controls

| Where | Button | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Map | ◄ ► | Choose the next room |
| Map | ▲ ▼ | Scroll the Spire |
| Map | A | Walk to the room |
| Combat | ◄ ► | Select a card |
| Combat | A | Play the card (then pick a target when several enemies are alive) |
| Combat | B | Cancel targeting |
| Combat | R | End the turn (L with the *Quick* control preset) |
| Combat | L | Potion belt (R with the *Quick* control preset) |
| Map / combat | START | Pause menu |
| Map / combat | SELECT | Deck viewer (B to go back) |
| Menus | A / B | Confirm / cancel |

The control preset and the music and sound volumes are in the options (SELECT on the title screen).

## Ascension

Every win unlocks the next Ascension level for that hero, up to 10. The handicaps add up:

| Level | Handicap |
|---|---|
| A1 | More frequent elites |
| A2 to A4 | Monsters, then elites, then bosses deal more damage |
| A5 | Less healing after a boss |
| A6 | Start with 10% less HP |
| A7 to A9 | Monsters, then elites, then bosses have more HP |
| A10 | The Ascender's Bane curse in your starting deck |

## Saving

The run is saved automatically on the cartridge (SRAM) at every room, with your settings. Choose *Continuer*
(continue) on the title screen to pick it back up. The title menu also has the *Compendium* (the cards, relics and
monsters you have met) and your run statistics.

## A few tips

- Not every card reward is worth taking: a lean deck draws its best cards more often.
- Elites hurt early in act 1. Fight one only when your deck can take it, and plan your path toward campfires.
- Upgrade the cards you play every fight first.
- Read the enemy intents before you commit your energy: sometimes the best turn is all Block.
