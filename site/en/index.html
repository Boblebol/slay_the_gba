Play in 3 minutes

  1. Download your hero's cartridge (a .gba file) from the Download section or the hero's page.
  2. Install a free Game Boy Advance emulator:
    • Computer (Windows, macOS, Linux): mGBA (recommended).
    • iPhone / iPad: Delta.
    • Android: My Boy! or RetroArch.
  3. Open the .gba file in the emulator (File → Load ROM, or import it in the app), then press START.
  4. Got real hardware? Copy the ROM to a flash cart (EZ-Flash, EverDrive) and play on GBA, GBA SP or DS.

FAQ

Is the game in English?

Not yet: every text in the game (cards, relics, events, menus) is in French. This English site gives the English names and texts of the cards, relics and potions, so you can look them up while you play, and the card art, numbers and icons make most of the game readable if you know Slay the Spire. An English version is possible if there is enough interest: tell me!

Which edition should I start with?

The Ironclad: 80 HP, a relic that heals after every fight and a straightforward deck. The Silent and the Defect ask for more planning; the Regent and the Necrobinder, new in Slay the Spire 2, are for seasoned players.

What changes from one cartridge to the next?

The hero, their cards, starting relic, signature mechanic, colour, zone and palettes. The Spire, monsters, shared relics, potions, events and Ancients are the same in all five editions.

Is it free?

Yes. The five ROMs are homebrew builds you can download for free. No commercial ROM is distributed.

Is my run saved?

Yes, automatically on the cartridge (SRAM) at every room, with your volume settings. Pick Continuer on the title screen to resume.

Can I play on a real Game Boy Advance?

Yes: copy the ROM to a flash cart (EZ-Flash, EverDrive) and play on GBA, GBA SP, Game Boy Micro or DS.

Is this an official game?

No. Slay the GBA is an unofficial, non-commercial fan game with no ties to Mega Crit or Nintendo.
