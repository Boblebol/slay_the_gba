The 5 editions

One hero per cartridge. Same Spire, five ways to climb it.

Ironclad Edition · Blood Red · Carnage Forges

The Ironclad — Cursed warrior from Hell

“Brute strength, forged in blood.”

The ideal hero to discover the Spire: lots of HP, a relic that heals after every fight and attacks that hit hard. Stack Strength, tank behind your Block, then crush everything.

80 HP · Relic: Burning Blood · Strength & sacrifice · ★☆☆ Beginner friendly

Silent Edition · Venom Green · Venom Marsh

The Silent — Deadly huntress of the mists

“Strike unseen. Let the venom finish the job.”

Fewer HP, but a bigger hand from the first turn thanks to Ring of the Snake. Poison, dodge and drown the enemy in shivs: the Silent wins by attrition.

70 HP · Relic: Ring of the Snake · Poison & shivs · ★★☆ Intermediate

Defect Edition · Sapphire Blue · Astral Complex

The Defect — Awakened combat automaton

“One automaton, three slots, endless circuits.”

The engineer's hero: every channeled orb works for you, turn after turn. Raise your Focus, line up lightning and frost, and watch the machine run away with the fight.

75 HP · Relic: Cracked Core · Orbs & Focus · ★★☆ Intermediate

Regent Edition · Solar Gold · Sovereign Palace

The Regent — Fallen celestial sovereign

“A crown, the stars, and the Spire on its knees.”

New in Slay the Spire 2, the Regent plays with two resources: energy and Stars. Divine Right gives him some from the start of the fight. Prepare your decrees, forge your blade, strike like a king.

75 HP · Relic: Divine Right · Stars & Sovereign Blade · ★★★ Expert

Necrobinder Edition · Spectral Violet · Beyond-the-Grave Crypt

The Necrobinder — Keeper of the dead and the bones

“She never fights alone.”

The lowest HP in the Spire, but a skeleton bodyguard who grows every turn thanks to Bound Phylactery. Protect Osty, mark your prey with Doom, and let death do its work.

65 HP · Relic: Bound Phylactery · Osty & Doom · ★★★ Expert
