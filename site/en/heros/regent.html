The Regent

“A crown, the stars, and the Spire on its knees.”

New in Slay the Spire 2, the Regent plays with two resources: energy and Stars. Divine Right gives him some from the start of the fight. Prepare your decrees, forge your blade, strike like a king.

Fallen celestial sovereign

Sovereign of a forgotten stellar dynasty, the Regent watched his celestial kingdom collapse under the Spire's grip. Clad in purple and gold, he wields a sceptre that channels the constellations and means to take his throne back.

Ideal for players who like managing one more resource and setting up a decisive turn.

Zone: Sovereign Palace · Cartridge colour: Solar Gold

Signature mechanic: Stars & Sovereign Blade

The Regent gathers Stars (★), a second resource on top of energy that fuels his strongest cards. He also Forges the Sovereign Blade: every "Forge" card makes it sharper, up to the royal strike that turns the fight around.

Survival tips

  1. Divine Right gives 3 ★ at the start of every fight: spend them early, don't let them sleep.
  2. Falling Star costs 0 and applies Weak and Vulnerable: the Regent's best opener.
  3. "Forge" cards add up: Conqueror then doubles the Sovereign Blade's damage.
  4. Keep an eye on energy AND Stars: a turn without one or the other is a wasted turn.
