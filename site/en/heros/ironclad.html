Cursed warrior from Hell

Last survivor of a decimated warrior order, the Ironclad sealed a pact with demonic powers to avenge his brothers in arms. Wielding a colossal blade forged in blood and blackened iron, he climbs the Spire to reach its heart.

Ideal for players who like to hit hard, take hits and have their mistakes forgiven by healing.

Zone: Carnage Forges · Cartridge colour: Blood Red

Signature mechanic: Strength & sacrifice

Strength raises the damage of every attack for the whole fight. The Ironclad stacks it (Inflame, Demon Form), trades HP for power and Exhausts his weak cards to sharpen his deck. Burning Blood closes his wounds after every victory.

Survival tips

  1. Open with Bash: Vulnerable makes the enemy take 50% more damage, then follow up with your Strikes.
  2. Burning Blood heals 6 HP after every fight: taking a little damage to end a fight a turn earlier often pays off.
  3. At the campfire, upgrade Bash and your Strength cards first: they boost all your attacks.
  4. Against groups, favour attacks that hit ALL enemies (Cleave, Thunderclap).
