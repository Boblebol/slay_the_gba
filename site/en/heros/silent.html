The Silent

“Strike unseen. Let the venom finish the job.”

Fewer HP, but a bigger hand from the first turn thanks to Ring of the Snake. Poison, dodge and drown the enemy in shivs: the Silent wins by attrition.

Deadly huntress of the mists

A feared huntress from the misty lands, the Silent hides her face under the skull of a horned beast. Where warriors rely on strength, she relies on agility, blades hidden under her cloak and toxins that consume her prey from within.

Ideal for patient players who like combos, card draw and damage over time.

Zone: Venom Marsh · Cartridge colour: Venom Green

Signature mechanic: Poison & shivs

Poison deals its damage at the start of the enemy's turn, then goes down by 1: it ignores Block and stacks up. Shivs are 0-energy attacks her cards create, perfect with Accuracy. Draw and discard cycle her deck faster than anyone else's.

Survival tips

  1. Neutralize on turn one: Weak reduces the enemy's damage by 25%.
  2. Poison ignores Block: it is your best answer to enemies who turtle up.
  3. With Ring of the Snake you start every fight with 7 cards: look for your key pieces from turn 1.
  4. Accuracy + Blade Dance turns every Shiv into a real attack.
