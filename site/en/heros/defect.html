Awakened combat automaton

An automaton built to purge the Spire, the Defect grew a mind of its own after its targeting subsystems failed. Powered by a cracked core, it keeps orbs of lightning, frost, darkness and plasma orbiting its chassis.

Ideal for players who like to plan, optimise and watch an engine run by itself.

Zone: Astral Complex · Cartridge colour: Sapphire Blue

Signature mechanic: Orbs & Focus

The Defect Channels Orbs into its slots. Each orb acts on its own at the end of the turn (Lightning: damage, Frost: Block, Dark: charge, Plasma: energy) and releases a stronger effect when it is Evoked or pushed out by a new orb. Focus strengthens every orb at once.

Survival tips

  1. Cracked Core channels a Lightning orb from the start: play Dualcast early for a big burst.
  2. Frost orbs make free Block every turn: two or three are enough to stabilise a fight.
  3. Focus (Defragment, Biased Cognition) is the stat that takes the deck from good to unplayable for the enemy.
  4. Capacitor adds slots: more orbs, more passive effects.
