Keeper of the dead and the bones

Bound forever to the Spire's wandering souls, the Necrobinder walks escorted by Osty, a skeletal familiar she raises again and again. Frail of body, she makes up for it with a twofold presence on the battlefield.

Ideal for players who like summons, delayed effects and high-risk runs.

Zone: Beyond-the-Grave Crypt · Cartridge colour: Spectral Violet

Signature mechanic: Osty & Doom

The Necrobinder fights alongside Osty, her skeletal familiar: "Summon" gives him HP, he takes hits in her place and strikes with Unleash. Doom marks enemies for certain death, and the Souls added to her Draw Pile feed her spells.

Survival tips

  1. Bound Phylactery summons at the start of every turn: the longer the fight, the sturdier Osty gets.
  2. Unleash adds Osty's current HP to its damage: let him grow before you let him loose.
  3. With 65 HP every point counts: let Osty take the big elite attacks.
  4. Doom (Deathbringer, Countdown) finishes off enemies whose HP drops below it.
